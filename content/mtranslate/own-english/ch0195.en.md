### 7. Register

Three distinct registers run through this chapter and must stay distinct in
Burmese:

1. **The crowd's dialogue** — colloquial, alarmed, inside quotation marks
   ([007]–[009], [035]–[036]).
2. **Ye Ning's inner voice** — modern, comic, gambling-and-internet slang:
   哥們, 大聰明, 加註, 這波很賺 ([062]–[078], [129]–[131], [193]–[195]). This
   is the one place in the book where a modern register is deliberate. It must
   **not** be formalised in the Burmese draft, and it must not leak into the
   narration around it.
3. **Ye Ning's public speech** — the starfish parable and the closing
   exhortation ([152]–[164], [185]–[188]), which is performed sincerity:
   elevated, cadenced, quotation-marked.

The whole comic effect of the chapter depends on 2 and 3 being in the same man
